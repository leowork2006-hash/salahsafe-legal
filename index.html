Support

Set a support email in scripts/build-legal-pages.js and re-run it before submitting to the App Store.

You can also use Report a Problem on the App Store listing.

Common questions

The Fajr alarm did not ring. Check Settings → SalahSafe → allow alarms, and that the alarm is switched on in the app. No alarm on iOS rings while the phone is powered off.

The camera will not accept the object. Get closer, turn on the light with the torch button, or tap "Give me something else". The waking duas will also unlock it.

Restoring a purchase. Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple ID that bought it.
